What I do

My work is the platform that ingests billing data from fourteen provider organisations — one parser strategy per source so onboarding a client adds files instead of editing the ones that already work, and a rules engine on top that opens a task when a record needs a human and closes it when later data proves the problem is gone.

Underneath that was a migration: a chain of stored procedures became an event-driven flow, where each step reacts to a recorded fact rather than being called by the step before it. You buy a trace of what happened and failure isolation; you pay with transactions you no longer get for free.

Alongside it I am doing an MSCS at Georgia Tech in machine learning — mostly to know precisely which side of the line a problem sits on: the conditions you can write down, and the patterns nobody can.
